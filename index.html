PROJECT 01 · STOCHASTIC GAMES

Tracking asymmetric incentives in Brownian Boost

A high-precision study of the equilibrium incentive function M: how far player incentives can diverge, and how its shape changes along a singular parameter limit.

↗ PROJECT 02 · QUANTUM SIMULATION

Scaling quantum simulation to thousands of states

A statewise POD-Galerkin method replaces one growing dense solve with a sequence whose measured cost is near-linear in the number of states, while preserving accuracy through 2,990 states.

↗ PROJECT 03 · REINFORCEMENT LEARNING

Building a 2048 agent from scratch—and with Keras

Two actor–critic implementations: a hand-built neural-network framework with derived convolutional and dense backpropagation, followed by a TensorFlow/Keras agent that reaches 2048.

↗ PROJECT 04 · MONTE CARLO

Counting legal Battleship boards with Gibbs sampling

A statistical-mechanics identity turns a difficult combinatorial count into an integral of sampled mean energies, validated on both small and full-size boards.
